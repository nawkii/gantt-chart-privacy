# Privacy policy

Gantt Chart - Offline, version 1.0.0. Effective November 13, 2026.

Gantt Chart is a project planner that works on your PC. Privacy is the reason it exists, so this policy is short: your projects never leave your PC, and the app collects nothing about you.

## What stays on your PC

- **Your projects.** Each project is a file with the ending .gantt, saved where you choose (new ones go to Documents\Gantt Charts unless you pick another folder). The app opens and saves those files and nothing else.
- **Earlier versions of your projects.** When a save replaces a project file, the app keeps the version it replaced in its own folder, so you can look at it or put it back (Project menu, Version history). It also keeps one recovery copy of each project in case the file is lost.
- **Templates you keep.** A project you keep as a template is a file in the app's own folder.
- **The list of projects you opened,** which ones you pinned, and how you last looked at each (zoom, columns).
- **Your settings.**
- **A counter for the review prompt:** how many exports you have made and when the app last asked for a review.
- **Your plan:** the last answer the Microsoft Store gave about your purchase, so Pro keeps working when you are offline, and a license key if you enter one. A key is checked on this PC; nothing is sent to check it.

Files you bring in (a Microsoft Project XML file, an Excel workbook, a CSV file, a board exported from Trello, a calendar file) are read on this PC. The file itself is never changed.

The app's own folder holds everything in this list except your project files. In the Microsoft Store version, Windows removes that folder when you uninstall the app. Your project files stay where you saved them.

## What Gantt Chart sends

Nothing about you or your projects. There is no account, no cloud, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not update itself (the Microsoft Store does that) and does not download anything while it runs: fonts, templates and the public holidays of every country it knows are installed with it. To put your own country first in that list, the app asks Windows which region the PC is set to; the answer stays on the PC.

The app makes contact with something outside your PC in only four ways, and only these:

1. **The Microsoft Store,** through Windows' own Store service, to check whether you own Pro (shortly after the app starts, when your PC wakes from sleep, when a plan is about to renew, and when you choose Check purchases), to show the current prices (only when you open the plan dialog or the Plan section of Settings), and to complete a purchase you start. Gantt Chart sends no data of its own in these requests. Microsoft handles them under the Microsoft Privacy Statement (privacy.microsoft.com).
2. **Manage subscription** opens account.microsoft.com/services in your web browser, only when you click it.
3. **Leave a review** opens Gantt Chart's page in the Microsoft Store app, only when you click it.
4. **Send feedback** opens a new email in your own mail app, addressed to the developer, with a subject line and nothing else. You decide what to write and whether to send it.

## Your control

- A project is a file: copy it, move it, back it up or delete it like any other file.
- Exports (PDF, image, SVG, Excel, CSV, Microsoft Project XML, a calendar file, a copy of the project to send) are saved only where you choose. Copy as image puts a picture on the Windows clipboard, a status update puts its text there, and Print hands the pages to the printer you pick.
- If you keep your projects in a folder that a sync service watches (OneDrive, Dropbox), that service copies them under its own terms. Gantt Chart has no part in it and does not know about it.
- Uninstalling the app removes its folder, with the earlier versions and the settings in it.

## Email

If you write to the developer, your message and email address are used only to answer you, and are not shared with anyone. You can ask for your emails to be deleted at any time.

## Children

Gantt Chart is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app (Settings > About > Privacy policy) and is published at the same address, with a new effective date. A change that would send anything new off your PC would be said plainly in the app before it took effect.
